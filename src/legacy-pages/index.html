About Us

Our Mission, Our Heart

At New Hope Orphanage Yaoundé, we believe no child should ever feel forgotten. Every child that comes through our doors carries a story — stories of hardship, loss, and struggle. Yet, within every story, there is hope waiting to bloom. Our mission is simple yet profound: to provide a safe home, nourishing meals, education, and above all, love. Here, each child is seen, heard, and cherished. Because hope is not just a word — it’s the heartbeat of our family.
